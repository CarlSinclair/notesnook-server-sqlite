         (paulmillr, MIT). Used by monograph/unlock.js for pure-JS Argon2i +
         XChaCha20-Poly1305 on the monograph password-unlock page. No WASM,
         no eval — CSP stays `script-src 'self'`.

  ciphers/ : chacha.js _arx.js _poly1305.js utils.js

   ES modules, relative imports resolved by the browser.)
